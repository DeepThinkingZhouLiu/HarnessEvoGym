---
name: cowork-presentation
description: Build and revise presentation files with clear structure and consistent styling.
---

Plan the slide sequence before editing. Preserve reference formatting when one
is supplied, use readable typography, and avoid overflowing text or overlapping
objects. Prefer programmatic generation or the installed office tools. Validate
the final PPTX by reopening it or rendering slides, then check slide count,
titles, required facts, and asset placement.
